About

I'm a Technical Lead at BMO working on high-availability systems design and practical AI-augmented engineering practices. My work spans SRE tooling, IoT infrastructure, and community data projects where the stakes are real and the audience is actual neighbours.

AI tools such as Claude, GitHub Copilot, and AWS Bedrock are useful force multipliers for infrastructure work. I still steer the architecture, security, and reliability decisions, while using AI to speed up implementation, analysis, and review. Projects like CFOperator push this further: scheduled agents for investigations and daily briefs, RAG over past incidents, and keeping a human on the merge button.
